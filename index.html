About the Hospital

A neighbourhood hospital that knows its patients by name

Located on the 1st floor of Kamla Amrut Apartment, opposite Rais Arcade on Manor–Palghar Road, Shinde Maternity and General Hospital serves families who earlier had to travel far for delivery and women's care. The focus is simple: honest advice, clear explanation in Marathi, and treatment without unnecessary fear or cost.

  • ✓Explained in your language — reports, medicines and delivery plan explained in Marathi / simple Hindi.
  • ✓For the whole family — maternity for mothers, general OPD for everyone else in the family.
  • ✓Easy to reach — on Manor–Palghar Road, convenient from Manor, Wada side and nearby villages.

FAQ

Common questions

Where exactly is the hospital?

1st Floor, Kamla Amrut Apartment, Manor–Palghar Road, opposite Rais Arcade Building, Palghar – 401403. Tap 'Directions' below to open it in Google Maps.

Do I need an appointment for pregnancy check-up?

Calling or sending a WhatsApp enquiry first helps reduce waiting time. For any emergency or labour pain, come directly and call on the way: +91 88570 22576.

What should I bring for the first pregnancy visit?

Any previous reports, sonography films, Aadhaar card, and a list of medicines you are currently taking, if any.

Is general treatment available for non-pregnancy patients?

Yes — this is a maternity AND general hospital. General OPD is for the whole family. Exact OPD timings will be confirmed by the hospital (demo website).
